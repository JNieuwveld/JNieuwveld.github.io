I am a Glasstone Research Fellow in Computer Science at the
[University of Oxford](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/) and a non-stipendiary Junior
Research Fellow at [Wadham College](https://www.wadham.ox.ac.uk/). I study decidability problems for linear recurrence sequences and for arithmetic
theories such as Büchi arithmetic.

Before Oxford, I did my PhD (2021–2025) at the
[Max Planck Institute for Software Systems](https://www.mpi-sws.org/) under the
supervision of [Joël Ouaknine](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/). Earlier, I
completed a bachelor's and master's in mathematics at Radboud University Nijmegen.
